Our Story

Riddhi Siddhi PG, a brand new stay for girls in Viman Nagar, Pune, opened its doors in February 2024. We have since expanded with a new location in July 2024 in Sopan Nagar lane no 1 near Pheonix Mall, Vadgoansheri.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, comfortable and modern living space for girls in Pune, with all essential facilities that make you feel at home while pursuing your education or career.

Why Choose Us?

  • Prime location near Phoenix Mall with excellent connectivity
  • 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance
  • High-speed WiFi available round the clock
  • Daily cleaning and maintenance services
  • Nutritious food service available
  • Supportive community environment
